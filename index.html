Section 05 · Use Case Reel
Solo rider in every clip — no passenger. Survey data confirmed the pre-order profile uses the second seat for storage. Showing a passenger would misrepresent how the actual buyer intends to use the vehicle. Each clip is environment-first: the location is established before Deux enters the frame. Clips are deliberately short — 6 seconds each — to hold attention across the full reel. The suburban-to-urban range of environments reflects the confirmed audience (suburban resident commuting into a city).
